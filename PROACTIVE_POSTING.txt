Proactive posting

Railway variables:

PROACTIVE_ENABLED=true
PROACTIVE_CHANNEL_IDS=111111111111111111,222222222222222222,333333333333333333
PROACTIVE_HOURS=11,16,21
PROACTIVE_TIMEZONE=Europe/London
PROACTIVE_CHECK_SECONDS=180

Behaviour:
- One proactive image per configured channel per local day.
- Channel 1 uses hour 1, channel 2 uses hour 2, etc.
- The exact minute is pseudo-random between :05 and :54 and changes daily.
- No catch-up flood if the bot was offline during a slot.
- Neon records the completed channel/day slot so restarts do not duplicate posts.
- Normal conversation takes priority if the bot is already generating.
- Proactive posts do not ping anyone.
- The bot uses its normal recent text, memories, RAG, self-reference and external images.
